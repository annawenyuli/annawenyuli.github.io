Why this site exists

Tech news is abundant; coherent mental models are scarce. A company announces an agent, another launches an MCP server, a cloud vendor releases a runtime, and an incumbent signs three partnerships—but the information is usually consumed as isolated events.

This site asks a different question: how do the pieces connect? The goal is to turn scattered product news into a map of competitive position, technical dependency, distribution, business model, and strategic direction.
